# Battleground: Fantasy Warfare — Quick Start Rules Summary

Condensed reference derived from `docs/battleground-quick-start-rules.pdf`
(Your Move Games, Men of Hawkshold set). The quick-start booklet is a streamlined
subset of the main rulebook; see `battleground-rules-summary.md` for the full
modifier tables and advanced rules.

## What you need

36″ × 30″ play area, about a dozen d6, a ruler/tape measure, and dry-erase markers.
The faction box supplies 70 unit cards, 30 command cards, and 2 reminder cards.
Win by destroying more than a points threshold (425 pts in the teaching scenarios)
of the opposing army.

## Turn structure (simplified)

1. **Movement & Command Phase** — active player gains 2 Command Actions (scenario
   rule), spends them, and moves units per their standing orders.
2. **Combat Phase** — both players' units attack; all attacks are simultaneous.
   Units pinched by a Final Rush this turn take a rout check first.
3. **Courage Phase** — units that took threshold damage roll rout checks; failed
   units rout and get attacked by engaged enemies.

## Command Actions (quick-start set)

Each costs 1 CA: Change Standing Order (not if engaged/already moved), Direct Control,
Draw a Command Card, Use Bravery ability (Hawkshold faction: mark the checkbox for
+3 Courage until a check is failed), Rally a routing unit. Unused CAs are lost.

## Standing orders

- **C — Close**: move toward the nearest visible enemy and engage in melee.
  Becoming engaged (either player's turn) counts as **charging** that turn.
- **R — Range**: shoot the nearest visible enemy; move toward it if out of range.
- **H — Hold**: stay put; still shoots/fights if able. Some units get bonuses
  when Holding (e.g. spearmen vs charging cavalry).

## Movement essentials

- Cards measure 3.5″ (long side, L) and 2.5″ (short side, S); movement distances map
  to card-side combinations (3.5″ = L, 5″ = SS, 6″ = LS, 7″ = LL, 8.5″ = LSS…).
- Move straight, turn (measure from the outside front corner), or maneuver.
- Maneuver penalties to Movement Category: Backwards −2, Sideways −1, About Face −1,
  Reform −2 (cumulative).
- Units can't move through or overlap other units; engaged units don't move.
- Measuring distances is always allowed, at any time.

## Final Rush (charging into contact)

Only units on Close. If a visible enemy's facing side is within the unit's Movement
stat (front center point to nearest enemy center point), pick the unit up and place
it in contact with as much of the enemy's facing side as possible. If the facing side
was blocked at the start of the turn, the unit may rush the nearest open side instead.
If two enemies are in reach, rush the nearest (you pick on ties).

## Attacking

1. Roll dice equal to the unit's Attack Dice stat (first attack stat, in parentheses).
2. **To-hit** target number = your Offensive Skill − enemy Defensive Skill.
   Each die at or under it is a hit.
3. **To-wound**: roll one die per hit; target number = your Power − enemy Toughness.
   Each die at or under it is a wound.
4. Mark one damage box per wound, left to right (Green → Yellow → Red).
5. A 1 always hits/wounds; a 6 always fails.
6. Ranged attacks: only unengaged units; nearest visible enemy within Range; can shoot
   over friendly units; mark an Ammo Box per shot — when all are marked, no more
   ranged attacks this game (the quick-start has no Reload).

During each attack, each player may play one Command Card (attacker chooses first).

## Combat modifiers (quick-start subset)

Notation `(±dice) ±OS/±OP`:

| Modifier | Value |
|---|---|
| Charging | (+2) +0/+0 |
| Pinching | (+0) +1/+1 |
| Flanking | (+0) +1/+0 |
| Rear Attacking | (+0) +1/+1 |
| Attacking to your Flank | (−1) −0/−0 |
| Attacking to your Rear | (−0) −1/−1 |
| In the Yellow | (−1) −0/−0 |
| In the Red | (−2) −0/−0 |
| Long Range | (−0) −1/−0 |
| Move and Shoot | (−0) −1/−0 |

Modifiers are cumulative (e.g. Pinching + Charging stack).

## Courage & routing

- Rout check triggers: marking the last Green or last Yellow box, or marking any Red
  box (multiple triggers in one phase = one check).
- Roll 3d6 ≤ Courage stat to pass. In the Yellow −1 Courage; In the Red −2.
- **Pinch check**: a unit engaged on 2+ sides where at least one pincher Final Rushed
  this turn takes a rout check before combat; failing routs it immediately.
- Failing a rout check: about-face and erase the order; all engaged enemies make a
  free attack (Rear Attacking applies); if it survives, it rout-moves toward its own
  table edge. Rushing the board edge destroys it. A routing unit that is Final Rushed
  is destroyed.
- Rallying costs 1 CA: reform the unit facing any direction with a Hold order; it
  can't move, attack, or change orders that turn.

## Teaching scenarios

1. **Insurrection!** — Grand Duke Bergan Temeraire (2 Spearmen, Swordsmen, Lancers)
   vs Baron Dinant (2 Militia, Peasant Mob, Scout Cavalry, Knights). All units on
   Close; 2 CAs per turn; lose at >425 pts destroyed. Baron goes first.
2. **Civil War!** (if the Baron won) — Bowmen/Spearmen/2 Great Swordsmen vs
   2 Militia/Communal Pikemen/Knights.
3. **Coup de Grace** (if the Duke won) — Militia/2 Spearmen/Communal Pikemen vs
   2 Militia/Longbowmen/Dismounted Knights.
